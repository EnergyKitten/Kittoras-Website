A safer server starts here

Safety & Security

We want Kittora to support welcoming, respectful Discord communities. Here are practical guidelines for bot users and server staff.

🛡️ Protect Your Server

  • Give Kittora only the permissions required for the features you use.
  • Limit sensitive moderation and configuration commands to trusted roles.
  • Review ticket visibility and logging settings to avoid exposing private conversations.
  • Regularly review staff access and server permissions.

🔐 Protect Your Account

  • Never share passwords, login codes, recovery codes, or bot tokens.
  • Be cautious with unexpected links and attachments.
  • Enable multifactor authentication on your Discord account.
  • Verify that bot invitations and announcements come from official channels.

💗 Community Safety

Do not use Kittora to bully, target, threaten, dox, or exploit other people. Report concerning behavior to your server moderators and use Discord's reporting tools when appropriate.

🚨 Report a Problem

To report abuse, a security issue, or a privacy concern, contact official Kittora Discord server. Include relevant IDs, timestamps, and a brief description, but do not share passwords or other secrets.

📌 A Note on Moderation

Automated moderation can make mistakes. Server staff should review important actions and provide a reasonable way to appeal where appropriate.
